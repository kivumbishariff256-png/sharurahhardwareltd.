About

 

 

Your trusted hardware partner since 2023

Welcome to Sharurah Hardware Ltd, Uganda's premier hardware supplier. For over a decade, we have been serving contractors, builders, DIY enthusiasts, and businesses with top-quality hardware products and exceptional customer service.

 

Our mission is to provide our customers with the best hardware products at competitive prices while maintaining the highest standards of quality and reliability. We stock a comprehensive range of products including construction materials, power tools, hand tools, plumbing supplies, electrical equipment, safety gear, and much more.

 

At Sharurah Hardware Ltd, we pride ourselves on our knowledgeable staff who are always ready to assist you in finding the right products for your projects. Our commitment to customer satisfaction has made us a trusted name in the hardware industry across Uganda.

 

We accept mobile money payments through MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money, making it convenient for our customers to shop and pay from anywhere. Our online platform allows you to browse products, manage your cart, and complete purchases with ease.
